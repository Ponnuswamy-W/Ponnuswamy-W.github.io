The publisher's heart

Turning classical Tamil thought into reading experiences for children

Posumi Veliyeedu is more than a page for one book. It is designed as a meeting point for language, ethics, social awareness, and a child's imagination.

Mission

To simplify the ethical ideas and social vision hidden inside classical works and present them in rich Tamil so that children and young readers can understand them early in life.

Objective

To gently move modern minds away from a narrow self-centered outlook and toward a broader social view, showing through ethical literature how life may be lived with meaning and honour.

Motto

Ari

Know Tamil knowledge and heritage

Amai

Shape character and inner balance

Adai

Attain a meaningful and socially rooted life

First release

Aathichudi - Valluvam Vai Vilakkam - Uyir Varukkam

Built around the Uyir Varukkam sequence, this first release invites children into ethics and Tamil expression through a warm and approachable reading experience.

Simple reading for children, memorable visual storytelling, and a gentle path into Tamil ethical thought.

About the book

This book begins to explain the brief and memorable lines of Aathichudi in a way children can read, repeat, and retain. It brings letter-learning and life-learning into the same experience.
